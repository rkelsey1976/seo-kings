### Baseline read for Phase 1 pilot sequencing

The plan's original Phase 1 pilot list (single-family towns with ~80% boilerplate content) mostly checks out against real numbers — Chew Magna, Temple Cloud, Clutton, Timsbury, Westfield, Paulton, Frome, Melksham, Corsham, Shepton Mallet, Bradford-on-Avon, Peasedown St John are all at or near 0 clicks on their `/areas/*` page. **One exception worth flagging: Saltford is stronger than assumed** — `/areas/saltford` (2 clicks/152 imp) and `/website-designer-saltford` (3 clicks/30 imp, 10% CTR, position 9.53) are both genuinely performing. Recommend moving Saltford to Phase 2 (mid-tier) rather than Phase 1, since it has real rankings to protect, unlike the rest of the original pilot batch.

Also worth noting for Phase 2/3 sequencing: Radstock and Midsomer Norton's `/website-designer-*` pages (position 13.25 and 26.88) are meaningfully stronger than their `/areas/*` or `/local-seo/*` siblings — the opposite pattern from Bath (where `/areas/bath` is the strongest of its four). This town-by-town variation in which page family "owns" the traffic is exactly why per-town, not batch, migration and a hold-and-gate at every phase matters.
